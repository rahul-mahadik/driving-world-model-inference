# Development qualification and component diagnostics

This revision preserves the earlier discovery bundle and adds completed 32-scene development comparisons plus bounded component and memory diagnostics. The primary generation remains 25 frames, one observed frame, 576×1024, 50 steps, FP16 and decode chunk 14. No held-out result or final optimized performance claim is made.

- [Paired bootstrap metrics and descriptive generation observations](bootstrap.json): both compiled variants differ from upstream at strict zero tolerance. Mean LPIPS is about 0.000522 and mean RMSE about 0.002091 across 32 scenes. These compare generated output with generated upstream, not driving quality. The two compiled variants are bitwise identical in only 11/32 pairs. Separate blinded reviews are relative preservation checks, not safety validation.
- [Attention alternative](attention-operator.json): forced efficient SDPA executes all 18 observed shapes, but all 9 self-attention cases fail exact zero. The largest spatial case is 82.38ms versus native 64.75ms. Single-key cases are exact and sometimes faster. These synthetic-value microbenchmarks do not establish end-to-end performance.
- [Actual denoiser input comparison](compiler-operator.json): identical captured first-step inputs and unchanged RNG still produce different compiled/eager outputs(maximum absolute error 0.051758, RMSE 0.001724). This narrows the location of the difference without identifying its cause. The compiled call includes cold compilation.
- [Memory recovery](memory-recovery.json): the 600-second B5 and B6 runs timed out in denoising. A timeout does not establish OOM or a maximum feasible batch. The B16 screen explicitly raised CUDA OOM after60.39 seconds with no retry and verified cleanup.
- [Current-source native attention counters](ncu-hot-attention.json): one replayed B2-shape kernel has 255 registers/thread, 12.37% active warps, 38.95% SM and 47.98% DRAM throughput. This is synthetic-value kernel profiling, not whole-model utilization or an independent timing comparison.
- [Development-only decision](development-decision.json): agent-assisted reviews and the explicitly selected development criteria support advancing compiled conditioning reuse to the 18-variant study. This does not change historical strict failures or complete the remaining quality and timing gates.
- [Recorded-future proxy comparison](recorded-ground-truth.json): paired differences against real future pixels are development diagnostics. They do not establish physical feasibility or trajectory adherence.
- [Published file checksums](checksums.json)

All numeric projections are allowlisted and retain original artifact hashes. Raw images, videos, arrays, private ratings/mappings, machine identifiers, host logs and cloud details are excluded. Standard quantitative quality, candidate isolation, exact cache semantics, independent timing and held-out gates still apply. Old strict failures have not been reclassified under new tolerances.
